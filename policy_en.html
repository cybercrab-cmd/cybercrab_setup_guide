CyberCrab Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. General Provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") governs the collection, processing, storage, protection, and transfer of user data by the CyberCrab online service (website https://cybercrab.cc and Telegram bot @CyberCrab_cc_vpn_bot, hereinafter referred to as the "Service").

1.2. By using the CyberCrab Service, the User confirms their agreement with the terms of this Policy. If the User does not agree with this Policy, they must immediately stop using the Service.

2. Data We Collect

2.1. The CyberCrab Service collects only the minimum amount of data necessary to provide the Service, process payments, and deliver customer support:

  • Telegram account identifiers: numeric Telegram ID, public username (@username), profile display name, selected interface language, and referral identifier (if joining via a referral link).
  • Payment and billing metadata: date, amount, currency, status, and internal transaction ID, selected plan, and internal balance top-up and debit history. Bank card details and payment credentials are entered and processed exclusively by payment providers and are never stored on CyberCrab servers.
  • Technical information: IP address and basic headers (User-Agent, device/OS type) when accessing the Service's web pages, subscription creation and expiration dates, technical configuration identifier, aggregate metered traffic volume (for enforcing plan limits), and number of connected devices.
  • Support communication history: messages and materials voluntarily submitted by the User when contacting the bot's ticket system, Telegram support, or email support.

2.2. The CyberCrab Service does not request or collect passport details, document photos, or other excessive personal information, nor does it log the contents of user network traffic or browsing history.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

3.1. Collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • identifying the User's account and activating, renewing, and managing the selected subscription plan;
  • processing payments, crediting internal balances, handling refund requests, and preventing fraudulent transactions;
  • maintaining technical reliability, enforcing plan limits, and improving the quality of the Service;
  • communicating with the User (service notifications regarding subscription expiration, Service updates, and customer support responses).

4. Data Recipients and Third-Party Disclosure

4.1. The CyberCrab Administration does not sell or disclose User data to third parties, except to a limited set of recipients essential for operating the Service:

  • Payment providers and aggregators (including Platega, CryptoBot, Telegram Stars, and other integrated payment gateways) — receive technical payment parameters (amount, order/account ID, status) to process payments, send settlement notifications, and execute refunds.
  • Hosting, server infrastructure, and CDN providers — host servers, databases, and web pages and deliver Service configurations.
  • Telegram platform — processes messages and commands exchanged between the User and the @CyberCrab_cc_vpn_bot bot via the Telegram Bot API.
  • Authorized government authorities — strictly where required by applicable law and pursuant to a valid, mandatory legal request.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Deletion

5.1. Data is retained for the duration of the User's account as needed to fulfill obligations under the Terms of Service. After prolonged inactivity (more than 12 months), inactive account data is deleted or anonymized, except for financial transaction records required to resolve disputes and process refunds.

5.2. The User may request clarification or deletion of their data by contacting CyberCrab customer support.

5.3. The CyberCrab Administration implements reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure (encrypted connections, access controls, backups), but cannot guarantee absolute security for data transmitted over open communication networks.

6. Disclaimer

6.1. The User understands and agrees that transmitting information over the Internet carries inherent technical risks.

6.2. The CyberCrab Administration is not liable for any loss or disclosure of data caused by third parties or resulting from the User's failure to maintain the security of their Telegram account or device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

7.1. The CyberCrab Administration reserves the right to update this Policy. The current version, along with its last updated date, is permanently available at https://cybercrab.cc/policy_en.html and in the @CyberCrab_cc_vpn_bot Telegram bot.

7.2. Any revised version takes effect upon publication. Continued use of the Service after updates are published constitutes the User's acceptance of the revised Policy.
